FAQ
Is Realify free?

Yes. Realify is free and supported by ads. There are no in-app purchases and no account.

Why does it need to be my default phone app?

Caller ID, call screening and silent blocking are only possible for the phone app that handles your calls. You can switch back at any time in Android settings.

Does the app upload my contacts or call log?

No. Contacts, call history and message previews are processed on your device and are not uploaded.

Will it catch every spam call?

Realify uses a bundled spam list and heuristics to label suspicious numbers. It helps you decide, but no caller ID can guarantee every spam call is detected.
