Privacy Policy

App: Realify: Caller ID & Block (package com.realify.callerid.callblocker) Developer: Khanh Thanh LTD ("we", "us", "our") Effective date: October 23, 2026 Contact: khanhthanhltd@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Realify: Caller ID & Block (the "App") accesses, how it is used, who receives it, and the choices you have. By using the App you acknowledge the practices described here.

1. What the App does

Realify is a caller ID, spam-call screening and call-blocking app. When you set it as your default phone app, it can show the incoming and in-call screens, screen incoming calls, block numbers you choose, show your call history and contacts, look up a phone number, and (optionally) show message previews taken from notifications of other chat apps ("Unseen").

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. We do not ask for your name, email address or password inside the App.

3. Information that stays on your device

The App needs the following information to work. It is read and processed locally; we do not receive it.

Information Android permission / access Why it is used
Phone state and the number of an incoming or outgoing call READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE, MANAGE_OWN_CALLS, default phone app role (InCallService and CallScreeningService) To show the incoming/in-call screen, identify the caller, and decide whether to allow, warn about, or silently reject a call.
Call history (call log) READ_CALL_LOG To show your recent, missed, incoming and outgoing calls in the History tab and in a contact's profile.
Contacts READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS To show contact names on calls and in lists, flag risky numbers, and let you add a new contact or photo from the App.
Placing and answering calls CALL_PHONE, ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS To let you dial, answer and end calls from the App.
Contact photo (optional) Android photo picker or the device camera app (no storage or camera permission) When you add a new contact in the App, you may pick one image with the system photo picker or take a picture with your device's camera app. Only the image you select or capture is made available to the App, and it is saved to the new contact on your device.
Block list and recent lookups Stored in the App's private storage To remember numbers you blocked and numbers you looked up.
Message previews ("Unseen") Notification access (notification listener) that you enable in Android settings To read the sender and text preview of notifications from supported chat and messaging apps and show them in the Unseen tab. Up to 500 recent previews are kept in the App's private storage on your device.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_PHONE_CALL To show the incoming-call screen and an ongoing-call notification.
Device features WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE To wake the screen for a call and vibrate.

Call log and phone permissions. The App uses call log and phone permissions only because it is registered as a phone (dialer) app, and only for the call features described above. We do not use call log, contacts or phone-number data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Message previews. Notification access is optional and is controlled by you in Android Settings > Notifications > Device & app notifications. The App only handles notifications from a fixed list of supported messaging apps and ignores other notifications. Previews are not sent off your device by the App. You can turn off access at any time in Android settings; stored previews are removed when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

Spam detection. The App ships with an offline database of reported spam numbers (derived from public community and government complaint data) and uses on-device scoring. Your lookups are not sent to us to be matched.

Device backup. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system (not us) may back up the App's local data, such as your block list, to your Google account according to your device settings.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the third-party service providers listed in Section 4, which process it for the purposes above, and with authorities when required by law. We do not share your call history, contacts, block list or message previews with anyone, because they do not leave your device through the App.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data (block list, recent lookups, cached profiles, message previews, settings) stays on your device until you clear the App's storage, remove an item inside the App, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes it.
  • Third-party data (analytics, crash, ads, attribution) is retained by each provider under its own policy. Because the App has no account and we cannot identify you from this data, we generally cannot look up an individual's records, but you can write to us at the address below and we will help to the extent possible, including passing on a deletion request. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Since there is no account, there is no "delete my account" step. To remove everything the App holds on your device, clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Realify: Caller ID & Block > Storage or uninstall it.

9. Security

Data that the App stores is kept in the App's private storage on your device. Network traffic from the App and its SDKs uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with a screen lock.
